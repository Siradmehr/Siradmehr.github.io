I am an exchange research student at ETH Zurich in Niao He's ODI group, working on the topology of diffusion models. Alongside this, I work with EPFL's LWE lab on agentic AI for photonic design and scientific workflows. I am also pursuing a master's degree in Statistics at the University of Geneva.

I graduated from Sharif University of Technology with a degree in Electrical Engineering. I was admitted to the Statistics PhD program at Columbia University, but my plans were interrupted by the travel ban.

My research goal is to develop a rigorous mathematical understanding of modern machine learning systems: to identify their limits, make them more robust and safe, and use theoretical insights to improve them. Guided by this goal, my current work focuses on diffusion processes, machine unlearning, differential privacy, and robust statistics.

In my free time, I enjoy following sports. I'm a huge fan of Bayern Munich, the Golden State Warriors, Red Bull Racing, and the New York Yankees.
